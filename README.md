## When does H&M actually have sales?

H&M runs a near-continuous cycle of seasonal promotions on top of its always-on sale section. Based on the sale calendar tracked by deal sites and the seasonal events listed on TopCashback's H&M page, the recurring pattern looks like this:

| Sale event | Typical timing | Reported discount depth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New Year / winter clearance | early January | up to 70% off |
| Valentine's promotion | around Feb 10–14 | up to 25% off |
| Mid-Season Sale | mid-to-late March | up to 50% off |
| Summer Sale launch | late June onward | up to 70% off |
| Back-to-School | August | up to 40% off |
| Fall promotion | October | up to 50% off |
| Black Friday | late November | see note below |
| Cyber Monday | the Monday after | up to 60% off, online |
| End-of-year clearance | Dec 26–31 | up to 70% off |

One honest caveat: sources disagree on Black Friday depth. TopCashback's offers page says Black Friday runs up to 30% off sitewide, while some third-party sale calendars list much higher headline numbers for the same weekend — and one of those calendars contradicts itself between its own chart and FAQ. What the sources do agree on: Cyber Monday goes deeper (up to 60% off) and is online-only. If you're shopping H&M once during the holidays, Cyber Monday is the safer bet.

Treat all of these as recurring patterns from trackers, not dates H&M officially guarantees. The permanent sale section is the only thing you can count on being there every single day. There are also smaller, less predictable events: Flash Friday Sales with 25% off select items from noon to 7 p.m. ET, and free-shipping weekends with no minimum.

## Can you stack H&M discount codes on sale items?

Usually, no. H&M generally allows one discount per order, and most promotional codes explicitly exclude already-reduced items. There are narrow exceptions — members can sometimes combine a bonus reward voucher with another discount, and during big events like Black Friday the sitewide discount has been reported to apply on top of existing markdowns — but as a planning assumption, treat codes and sale prices as either/or.

Three more exclusions that trip people up: gift cards and designer collaborations are typically excluded from promotions entirely; the student discount (10% off full-price items via Student Beans or UNiDAYS) doesn't apply to sale merchandise; and TopCashback only honors codes published on its own H&M page — a code pulled from somewhere else can get your cash back declined. ## Returns on sale items: the 30-day rule and "Final Sale"

H&M's standard return window is 30 days from purchase (for online orders, from delivery), with items unworn, unwashed and tags attached. No direct exchanges — you return and reorder.

The catch specific to sale shopping: **items marked "Final Sale" can't be returned or exchanged at all.** These are usually the deepest-markdown, last-chance pieces. That's the trade for the season's lowest price. If there's real uncertainty about fit, buying at an earlier markdown stage where returns are still possible often makes more sense, even for a few dollars more. Everything else in the sale section follows the normal 30-day policy, and returns do process reasonably fast in practice — one shopper quoted in TopCashback's reviews said she returned an order on January 4 and had the refund back in her account by January 7.
